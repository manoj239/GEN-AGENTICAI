"I built an AI-powered incident management workflow in n8n. Monitoring alerts from 
CloudWatch or Prometheus trigger the workflow through a webhook. The workflow collects
incident details and sends them to a Gemini-powered AI Agent, which analyzes logs and 
metrics to generate an RCA summary. If the analysis is successful, a Jira ticket is 
automatically created, stakeholders are notified through Slack or Teams, and the final 
incident report is stored for future reference.  This helps reduce manual analysis effort
 and improves incident response time.
