I built an AI-powered Incident Management workflow using n8n. The solution follows an event-driven
architecture where alerts from monitoring tools automatically trigger the workflow. goal would be 
to automate incident analysis, RCA generation, and stakeholder communication. Monitoring 
alerts from  CloudWatch or Prometheus trigger the workflow through a webhook. The workflow collects
incident details and sends them to a Gemini-powered AI Agent, which analyzes logs and metrics to
generate an RCA summary. If the analysis is successful, a Jira ticket is automatically created,
stakeholders are notified through Slack or Teams, and the final  incident report is stored for
future reference.  This helps reduce manual analysis effort  and improves incident response time.
